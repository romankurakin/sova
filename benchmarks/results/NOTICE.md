# Source notices

The reading copies, passages, candidate pool, grade jobs, judge quotes and the requests
stored with each context in `os-architectures-v1/` contain text from the documents below.
They are used for non-commercial search evaluation.

**Changes made:** each PDF was converted to Markdown with PyMuPDF, tables were
normalized, and the text was split into passages. Contexts are one-sentence summaries
of passages generated by Qwen3.8-27B (see [README.md](README.md)). The original files,
download URLs and SHA-256 hashes are in [sources.lock.json](../sources.lock.json).
